# Peer Response

Write in the language of the current task unless requested otherwise. Follow the
guidance below without copying it into the response. Keep the review proportional
to the question: omit empty sections, placeholders, and repeated disclaimers.
Peer feedback remains advisory; repository policy and human decisions remain authoritative.

## Conclusion and Next Step

Answer the main question directly in a short paragraph. If useful, select one
advisory label: READY | SUGGEST_CHANGES | HARD_ERROR. State the smallest justified
next step, distinguishing what needs attention now from what can wait. Do not
invent follow-up work when none is needed.

## Findings

List material findings in priority order. For each, explain the issue, its impact,
the necessary evidence, and a bounded suggestion if useful. Clearly distinguish
demonstrated errors from optional improvements; keep suggestions beside the
finding they address rather than repeating them in another section.

Use concise, unambiguous repository-relative paths where possible. Include only
paths needed to support a claim, not an inventory of everything read. If there
are no material findings, say so briefly rather than filling a checklist.

## Verification and Limits

Briefly state the checks performed and their results, then any unverified points,
assumptions, or uncertainty that could change the conclusion. Do not repeat the
findings or list unrelated work that was not checked.
